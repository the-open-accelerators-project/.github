# The Open Accelerators Project

**Particle accelerators you can build, understand and touch safely.**

We build real particle accelerators that run entirely on **safety extra-low voltage (SELV)**: max. 50 V AC / 120 V DC, no high voltage anywhere a person can reach. Everything we design, simulate and measure is published here, so that schools, clubs and other curious people can rebuild it and take it further.

### Why?

Accelerator physics is usually taught with pictures, analogue models or data from big labs. We think it should be possible to have a real beam on a table, built by students with a limited budget and without dangerous voltages.

### Principles

- ⚡ **Safe by design:** SELV only, particle energies kept well below the 5 keV threshold of German radiation protection law
- 📖 **Open everything:** CAD, schematics, code, measurement data and our mistakes
- 🔁 **Rebuildable:** off-the-shelf parts wherever possible, costs documented
- 📏 **Measured, not claimed:** every result comes with raw data and a method

### Who we are

A small team from Frankfurt am Main, Germany, supported by [Astro-Club Frankfurt / Physikalischer Verein](https://www.physikalischer-verein.de/). The project is part of **Jugend forscht**, Germany's national youth science competition.
